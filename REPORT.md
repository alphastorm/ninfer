## E2 integration cases

- C2 fanout and warm arrival: two sessions of one principal share stable/tool prefixes while retaining distinct private continuations; foreign principal/private attempts miss. Hold one response Publishing while the sibling triggers Host/state/KV pressure: Pending protects the entire release/demotion plan, then checkpoint progress wakes admission without decode-tick rescans.
- Save/restore overlap: hold checkpoint coordination during worker pressure; finish disk publication during a prepared restore; remove installed handler/oracle during callbacks. Require no lock inversion, no partially published native/Responses state, stale captured epoch never invokes current replacement, and RAII abort retires reservations.
- Capacity matrix: insufficient empty-host capacity; resident recoverable victim; missing/stale/corrupt/unavailable victim pin; generation replacement after byte verification; target retry with a fresh reader; all initial victims exhausted. Only genuine verified recoverability authorizes reclaim, bounded attempts never expand for arrivals, retryable I/O never quarantines, and refusal leaves non-reclaimable victims resident.
- Policy/identity matrix: warmup `cache_participation=false`, `allow_prefix_reuse=false`, global cache disabled, trusted-active engine/native/frontend mismatch and unsupported native codec. Policy must refuse before Reader/native work; unchanged capacities/retention are not fingerprint mismatches.
- Product proof: C2/131072 logical context/automatic BF16 KV/DFlash2 K7/1024 prefill/Vision/optimized proposal head/+2 Device StateImages/21881405440 pooled Host bytes. Batch keep-warm off/on idle-TTFT, native and ResourceManager regressions, startup baseline comparison, then ServePort2's real serving-coordinator restart with at least 16K checkpoint-hit tokens and byte-identical deterministic continuation. Public receipts contain only allowlisted identifiers, counts, timings, digests and relative filenames.
